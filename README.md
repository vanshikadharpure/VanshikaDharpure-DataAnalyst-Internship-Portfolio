## About This Portfolio

This repository brings together my complete 60-day Data Analytics Internship at ApexPlanet Software — from raw data to a fully validated, stakeholder-ready business story. ## Key Insight from the Internship

Across the analysis, Electronics and East India consistently led every revenue chart. Rather than taking this at face value, I tested it statistically — and found that this leadership comes from higher purchase **volume**, not higher spend per order. Pricing behavior is actually consistent across every segment tested. This finding shaped the final business recommendation: grow reach and purchase frequency in top segments, rather than assuming they can support premium pricing.
